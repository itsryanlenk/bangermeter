Bangermeter

Bangermeter is installed.

Three things and you're using it.

1

Scroll x.com

Every post picks up a badge with two numbers, 0–100.

Looks like C71 E48 C — what the content alone would be predicted to earn E — what the post actually earned, per view

50 is a typical post. It's a comparison against a median post, not a grade — and not a prediction of reach.

Open x.com →

2

Click a badge

You get the breakdown in plain English: which signals fired, what each reply and repost is actually worth, and what age cost it.

Every number is cited to the line of X's published code it came from. “Show the math” on any section opens the full weight × probability table.

3

Start typing a post

A live meter scores your draft as you write, and names what's helping and what isn't — asks a question, substantive text, bare link, hashtag pile, engagement bait.

Hit + compare to save up to three variants and pick the best one before you send it. Replies are scored as replies.

One thing worth knowing

The weights are X's. The probabilities are estimates.

Only likes, replies and reposts come from real counts — the rest is inferred from the post. The popup labels all 26 ranking heads as measured, estimated, or zeroed by X, so you always know which is which.

Settings, the full weight table and your score history live behind the toolbar icon — click the Bangermeter icon any time.
