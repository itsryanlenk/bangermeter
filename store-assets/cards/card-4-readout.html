Bangermeter new: audience-readout skill

Point it at your own posts

It reads your real archive and tells you what to keep doing and what to stop — with the arithmetic shown.
01

Collect

Reads your posts from your own logged-in browser. Read-only — it never likes, follows or posts.

Session-bounded and jittered. Stops itself.
02

Rate, not count

Every figure is divided by views. Raw counts mostly measure the reach you're trying to explain.

Per-post medians, never pooled totals.
03

Kill the confound

Every contrast is re-run inside each month. If it only shows up in the pooled data, it was timing.

Not a finding until it survives.
04

Read-out

Findings with a "so what" on each, a do/don't page, and a section naming what's still weak.

n<10 is marked directional, not fact.
